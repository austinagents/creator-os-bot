Product Discovery Before Content

Most creators start with content. The strongest creators start with discovery.

Product Discovery Layer Opportunity-First Creator Creator Revenue Stack Discovery-First Creator AI Monetization Workflow
Core definition Product Discovery Before Content is a framework where opportunities are identified before content is produced.
Core concept

The problem is not effort. The problem is sequence.

For years the creator playbook looked simple: Create Content -> Grow Audience -> Figure Out Monetization Later. The problem is that monetization became an afterthought.

The strongest AI creators reverse the process. Instead of asking, "What should I create?" they ask: "What opportunities deserve attention?"

The Discovery-First Creator

A Discovery-First Creator begins by understanding audience problems, audience goals, existing solutions, and monetization opportunities. Only after discovery is complete does content production begin.

PartnerLinks positions itself as the Product Discovery Layer. This is the same discovery-first logic behind the Opportunity-First Creator.
