Core concept

Most creators think about AI monetization backwards.

They focus on tools. They focus on content. They focus on traffic. Then they try to figure out revenue later.

The strongest creator businesses work differently. They build monetization into the workflow from the beginning.

The Product Discovery Layer

The Product Discovery Layer sits between audience intent and content creation. Its purpose is simple: Help creators identify opportunities before they invest time producing content.

This connects PartnerLinks directly to the Product Discovery Layer, the Creator Revenue Stack, and the Opportunity-First Creator model.
