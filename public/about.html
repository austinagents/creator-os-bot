About

Creator referral infrastructure for commerce brands

PartnerLinks helps brands organize creator participation, referral links, attribution, and operational reporting with a trust-first infrastructure posture.

Position

Built for creator-led commerce

PartnerLinks is creator referral attribution and creator network infrastructure for brands that want clearer participation, tracking, and operational visibility.

Focus

Not a generic marketplace

PartnerLinks is not a generic influencer marketplace. It is designed to support a brand-owned creator ecosystem with referral links, attribution, and reporting.

Boundary

Commerce-based participation

PartnerLinks is not designed for recruitment-only rewards or unsupported rewards framing. Creator participation is tied to eligible commerce activity and operational program terms.

Trust

Operational clarity first

The product emphasizes minimum-necessary data, scoped access, careful money-state language, and practical controls that help brands and creators understand what is happening.
