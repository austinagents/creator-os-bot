AI made content abundant. Discovery became scarce. PartnerLinks is the Product Discovery Layer.

Core concept

Why content is no longer the bottleneck

For most of the internet's history, creators had a production problem. Creating content was difficult. Writing articles took time. Recording videos took time. Research took time. Publishing consistently took time.

Then AI arrived. Today a single creator can generate articles, newsletters, video scripts, tutorials, social posts, research summaries, and educational resources faster than ever before.

The Content Trap

The Content Trap is the belief that more content automatically leads to more revenue.

Thousands of creators publish articles, newsletters, videos, tutorials, and social posts every single week. Many never generate meaningful revenue. Not because their content is bad. Because they never solved discovery.

The Opportunity-First Creator model and the Discovery-First Creator model both begin from this same shift: creators need to understand opportunities before they create.
